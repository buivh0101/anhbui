About Me

I am a graduate student at Boston College pursuing a Master's in Applied Econometrics, with a background in Business Analytics and Information Systems.

My main interest is data science with a focus on causal inference. I am interested in understanding not only what happens in data, but why it happens and how interventions, policies, or product decisions change outcomes.

My goal is to work in data science roles where causal inference, experimentation, and econometrics are used to guide real-world decisions.
